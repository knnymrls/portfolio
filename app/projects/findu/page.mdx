<CaseStudySection title="Overview">

FindU is a platform designed to help Generation Z students navigate the critical transition from high school to their next life chapter. With countless options available—from traditional college to trade schools, gap years, and direct employment—many students feel overwhelmed and uncertain about their path forward.

The platform leverages AI and personalized assessments to provide tailored recommendations and guidance, helping students make informed decisions about their future.

<CaseStudySection title="The Problem">

After graduating high school, students face a complex landscape of choices without clear guidance:

- **Information Overload**: Too many options without clear comparisons or personalized guidance
- **Pressure & Uncertainty**: Societal and family expectations often conflict with personal interests
- **Limited Self-Knowledge**: Students struggle to match their skills and interests with career paths
- **Accessibility Barriers**: Career counseling is often expensive and not available to all students

Traditional guidance counselors are overworked, averaging 482 students per counselor nationally, making personalized attention nearly impossible.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudyCallout type="info">

**Key Insight**: Students don't need more information—they need better guidance and a framework for making decisions that align with their unique strengths and goals.

</CaseStudyCallout>

<CaseStudySection title="The Solution">

FindU provides a comprehensive, AI-powered platform that guides students through self-discovery and decision-making:

### Personalized Assessment

A multi-dimensional assessment evaluating interests, skills, values, and goals to create a comprehensive student profile.

### AI-Powered Recommendations

Machine learning algorithms match students with pathways that align with their unique profile, from traditional colleges to alternative education and career paths.

### Interactive Exploration

Students can explore different pathways, compare options side-by-side, and understand the real-world implications of each choice.

### Action Planning

Once a direction is chosen, FindU provides step-by-step guidance on applications, requirements, and timelines.

</CaseStudySection>

A conversational assessment that feels less like a test and more like a discussion with a mentor. Questions adapt based on previous answers, keeping the experience relevant.

### Pathway Cards

Visual representations of different post-high school options with quick stats, estimated costs, time commitment, and career outcomes.

<CaseStudySection title="Challenges & Learnings">

### Challenge: Building Trust with AI

Students were initially skeptical of AI-generated recommendations. We addressed this by:

- Making the matching algorithm transparent
- Showing the "why" behind each recommendation
- Allowing students to override suggestions

### Challenge: Balancing Guidance with Autonomy

Finding the right level of direction without being prescriptive. Solution: Positioned FindU as a tool for exploration rather than a decision-maker.

</CaseStudySection>
